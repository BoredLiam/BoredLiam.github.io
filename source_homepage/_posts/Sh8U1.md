## Starting out

这章的主题是**自我认知**。通过自我认知我们能够了解自己，从而改变自己，实现进步。

<div style="font-family: sans-serif;">导语：
Imagine a <strong>towering</strong>, <strong>splendid</strong> castle. Each turn you make through it is a surprise. There are so many rooms that you could explore all day without seeing the same thing twice. Now, imagine that castle is the world within you. As a child, it was exciting to run around the halls, to crawl through the secret passageways, to spend the day pretending to be a wonderful cook in the kitchen or a professor of magic in the grand library. But as time went on, people expected you to sit politely in the dining hall or to make conversation in the drawing room. You began to lock doors. Those rooms eventually became dusty and forgotten. One day, you looked around and realised that your life in the castle was reduced to a few rooms. Where has the splendid castle gone? Luckily, those forgotten rooms have not disappeared, even if it has been some time since you last visited them. You still hold the key. If you are ready and willing, you have the power to unlock those doors and rediscover your whole self.
</div>

`towering` adj.高大的；高耸的；屹立的；卓越的
`splendid` adj.壮观的；华丽的；极好的；出色的
内心的城堡在一日日的成长中逐渐封闭，但是我们依旧可以找回以前的自我。
