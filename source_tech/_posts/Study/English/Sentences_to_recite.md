## *A.*倒装

1. <u>Never before had I</u> discovered the persistence within my body.
2. <u>Not until then did I</u> realize that a self-processed(≈*calm*) mind towards accidents can always turn negatives to positives.
3. <u>Only then did I</u> come to know the potential / internal power hidden in my positive mind.
4. <u>Only in this way can we</u> confront the challenge with bravery and confidence.
5. <u>By no means should these</u> minor efforts be ignored.
6. <u>On the table lies</u> a little note, with a little smile scribbled on it. (完全倒装)
7. <u>Shy as he was</u>, he managed to ...

## *B.*强调句

1. <u>It is</u> the genuine love <u>that</u> guides us through the mist and clouds, giving us confidence to overcome obstacles.
2. <u>It was not until then that</u> a sense of relief washed away the intense and nervous atmosphere.

## *C.*省略句

1. Though <u>lacking confidence</u>, he managed to present his first performance to the classmates.
2. He always keeps silent <u>unless asked</u>.
3. Jimmy wanted to jump down from the tree but was asked by his father <u>not to</u>.

## *D.*名词性从句

1. <u>What surprised me was</u> that during the run, I could feel my attitude towards it had changed.
2. <u>It occurred to him that</u> the shovel in the garage might be of some help.

## *E.*状语从句

1. <u>Told that</u> the boy had already been sent to the hospital, I finally let out a sigh of relief.
2. <u>Though surprised</u> to see us, they were willing to provide temporary accommodation for us.
3. <u>When a moment of fear went through my mind</u>, I told myself to calm down since I was still alive.

## *F.*无灵主语

1. <u>30 days of training witnessed</u> her change in both endurance and perseverance.
2. <u>A burst of pride seized her</u> on hearing the genuine praise from the audience.

## 附：词汇替换

- interesting → fascinating
- hope → anticipate
- interested in → passionate / keen on / fond of
- invite → extend an invitation to
